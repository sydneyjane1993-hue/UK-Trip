Senior citizen discounts – please read

The two seniors are 65 and 79, and are already included in the adult headcount. Discounts are applied only where the attraction confirms them.

Historic Royal Palaces – Tower of London and Kensington Palace
Visitors aged 65+ qualify for concession tickets, including international visitors, with proof of date of birth. Carry a passport or other accepted ID and show it at the ticket desk if asked.
Tower of London current published no-donation prices: adult £38; senior £30.30; ages 5–15 £19; under 5 free. This saves £7.70 per eligible senior before any optional donation.

Do not assume every UK attraction gives a senior discount. The ticket calculator uses a senior price only when one is published for that specific attraction. Carry proof of age and verify the accepted ID before purchase.
Senior Railcard – worth checking for the train journeys
Both seniors (65 and 79) meet the 60+ age requirement. Visitors to Britain can apply for a Senior Railcard, which saves 1/3 on eligible rail fares. Each traveller needs their own card; restrictions and eligible ticket types apply, so compare the saving with the card cost before buying. The official FAQ notes that some machine-readable passports issued in the Philippines before 2010 may not validate for online purchase. Check the official guidance or ask at a staffed station if online validation fails. Railcard savings are not included in the trip totals.
